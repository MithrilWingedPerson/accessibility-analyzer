A practical guide to making image descriptions and accessible alternatives more useful.

How to use this tool: Tell us how the image is used and what your audience needs to understand. The analyzer checks the information you provide and suggests next steps.

This is a learning aid, not an accessibility certification. It does not inspect the image itself or guarantee that content is accessible.
